--- Slide 10 ---
Summary
● A Graph is a set of nodes and set of edges
● Adjacency relations between nodes in graph encode the social ties or 
social connections in the network
● Adjacency is a pairwise relation (involves two nodes at time)
● Nonadjacent nodes are not directly connected (although they may be 
indirectly connected)
● Incidence relations between nodes and edges in graph encode the 
connection between people and the social ties they have
● Isolate nodes are disconnected from the rest of the network

--- Slide 108 ---
Summary of Concepts (Subgraphs, Neighborhoods)
● Subgraph: A subset of either nodes or edges from the larger graph.
● Node-deleted subgraph: Subgraph obtained from removing nodes.
● Edge-deleted subgraph: Subgraph obtained from removing edges. 
● Node neighbors: Set of nodes directly adjacent to another node.
● Neighborhood intersection: Set of neighbors shared by two nodes.
● Neighborhood union: Set of neighbors directly adjacent to two other 
nodes.
● Node degree: Number of nodes directly adjacent to another node.


--- Slide 109 ---
Summary of Concepts (Degree)
● Graph Degree Set: Vector (list) of the degrees of each node in a graph.
● Graph Degree Sequence: Ordered list of the degrees of each node in a 
graph from biggest to smallest.
● Graph Maximum Degree: Largest number in the graph degree set.
● Graph Minimum Degree: Smallest number in the graph degree set.
● Graph Degree Range: Diﬀerence between a graph’s maximum and 
minimum degrees.


--- Slide 110 ---
Summary of Concepts (Graph Metrics)
● Graph Sum of Degrees: Sum of each of the numbers in the graph degree 
set.
● Graph Average Degree: The sum of degrees divided by the graph order.
● Complete Graph: A graph in which every node is adjacent to every other 
node.
● Graph Maximum Size: Number of edges in a complete graph of a given 
order.
● Graph Density: Ratio of the observed number of edges in a graph (the 
graph size) to a graph’s maximum size
