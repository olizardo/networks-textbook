### I. Density: Undirected vs. Directed
*   Explain the formula difference:
    *   Undirected: $d = \frac{2m}{n(n-1)}$

### II. Reciprocity in Graphs
*   **Undirected Triviality**: Explain that undirected edges are symmetric by default, so reciprocity is always $100\%$.
*   **Directed Non-Triviality**: In digraphs, reciprocity measures the prevalence of mutual connections ($u \leftrightarrow v$).

### III. Reciprocity Metrics: Formulas
*   **Arc-based Reciprocity** ($r_{arc}$): Proportion of directed edges (arcs) that are part of a reciprocal relationship:
    $$r_{arc} = \frac{2 \times D_{rec}}{m}$$
*   **Dyad-based Reciprocity** ($r_{dyad}$): Proportion of connected vertex pairs (dyads) that are mutual:
    $$r_{dyad} = \frac{D_{rec}}{D_{rec} + D_{asym}}$$

### IV. Reciprocity Metrics: Example
*   Calculate both metrics using the 6-node digraph (`gr_dir`, $n=6, m=8$):
    *   $1$ mutual dyad ($A \leftrightarrow B$)
    *   $6$ asymmetric dyads
    *   $D_{rec} = 1$, $D_{asym} = 6$
    *   $r_{arc} = \frac{2 \times 1}{8} = 0.25$ ($25\%$) — corresponds to igraph `"default"`
    *   $r_{dyad} = \frac{1}{1 + 6} = \frac{1}{7} \approx 0.143$ ($14.3\%$) — corresponds to igraph `"ratio"`
