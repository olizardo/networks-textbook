## 1. Content of the Slide Deck

We will create a 10-slide presentation using the native Quarto RevealJS slide engine in `slides/cognitive-social-structures.qmd`, matching the styling and theme of the textbook (`ucla-theme.css`, 1280x720 RevealJS format).

### Slide Outline:
1.  **Title Slide:** "Cognitive Social Structures"
    *   Subtitle: "Perceptions, Slices, and Accuracy in Social Networks"
2.  **Introduction to CSS (Krackhardt, 1987):**
    *   The psychological dimension of social networks: networks as mental constructs.
    *   Contrasting standard network surveys (direct ego-alter relations) with CSS surveys (asking everyone about every pair $i \rightarrow j$).
3.  **The 3D Matrix Representation ($R_{N \times N \times N}$):**
    *   Mathematical formulation: $R_{ijk} = 1$ if person $k$ perceives a tie exists from $i$ to $j$.
    *   Visual representation of the 3D data cube (Individual Cognitive Slices $\mathbf{R}_{..k}$).
4.  **CSS Aggregations - locally Aggregated Structures (LAS):**
    *   Combining direct dyadic reports to approximate actual networks:
        *   **Intersection/Row LAS:** Agreement ($LAS^{\cap}_{ij} = R_{iji} \times R_{ijj}$).
        *   **Union/Or LAS:** At least one reporter ($LAS^{\cup}_{ij} = \max(R_{iji}, R_{ijj})$).
5.  **CSS Aggregations - Consensus Structures (CS):**
    *   Aggregating perceptions of the entire group:
        *   **Threshold Consensus ($\alpha$):** $CS^{\alpha}_{ij} = 1$ if $\frac{\sum_k R_{ijk}}{N} \ge \alpha$.
        *   **Majority Consensus:** Typically $\alpha = 0.50$.
6.  **Key CSS Metrics - Cognitive Accuracy:**
    *   Formally defining individual perceptual accuracy: QAP correlation between an individual's slice $R_{..k}$ and the group Consensus Structure ($CS$).
    *   Cognitive biases in networks (reciprocity bias, balance/transitivity bias, and ego bias).
7.  **Empirical Research 1: Structure, Cognition, and Power (Krackhardt, 1990):**
    *   The ASQ Silicon Systems study ($N = 36$ employees).
    *   **Core Finding:** Having an accurate cognitive map of the informal advice network is a significant predictor of reputed power, *even when controlling for actual centrality*. "Power belongs to those who know the structure."
8.  **Empirical Research 2: Simmelian Ties & Cognitive Inconsistencies (Krackhardt, 1999):**
    *   **Simmelian Ties:** Mutually reciprocated friendships that are embedded in mutual triads (cliques of size 3+).
    *   **Cognitive Inconsistencies (Carley & Krackhardt, 1996):** Discrepancies between perceived friendship and actual network reports, driven by cognitive balance pressure.
9.  **Summary of David Krackhardt's Contributions to SNA:**
    *   E-I Homophily Index (1988)
    *   Reachability, Connectedness, and Efficiency dimensions of informal organizations (1994)
    *   Simmelian Tie Theory (1999)
    *   CSS Methodology (1987, 1990)
