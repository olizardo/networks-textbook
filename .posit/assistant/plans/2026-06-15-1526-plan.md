# Plan: Consolidating and Self-Containing All Lecture Slides

This plan outlines a comprehensive, repository-wide visual audit and consolidation of all Reveal.js slide decks in the `/slides` folder. It addresses two primary goals:
1. **Self-Containment**: Ensuring that any slide performing mathematical calculations, exemplifying metrics, or describing graph properties contains the relevant R graph plot *directly* on that slide (eliminating "see previous slide" references).
2. **Structural Consolidation**: Merging split slides (those with only 3–4 bullets on the same subject) back into tight, high-impact slides, taking advantage of our newly implemented `scrollable: true` and typography optimizations.
