## 1. Visual Fixes & Slide Splits

### Step 1.1: Re-layout the Directed Triad Figures to 1-Row Ribbons
To prevent any vertical squishing in landscape slide aspect ratios, we will modify the grid layouts to be single-row horizontal ribbons:
* **The 7 Anti-Symmetric Triads (`@fig-antisym-triads-slides`)**:
  - Convert from 2 rows of 4 columns to a **single row of 7 columns**:
    ```r
    p1 + p2 + p3 + p4 + p5 + p6 + p7 + plot_layout(nrow = 1)
    ```
  - Set chunk options to `#| fig-height: 2.8` and `#| fig-width: 12`.
* **The 9 Reciprocal Triads (`@fig-recip-triads-slides`)**:
  - Convert from 2 rows of 5 columns to a **single row of 9 columns**:
    ```r
    p1 + p2 + p3 + p4 + p5 + p6 + p7 + p8 + p9 + plot_layout(nrow = 1)
    ```
  - Set chunk options to `#| fig-height: 2.8` and `#| fig-width: 12`.

### Step 1.2: Split `## Triads under Anti-Symmetric Ties` in Two
This slide has 9 total bullet points (including sub-bullets), which exceeds our 6-bullet rule. We will split it into:
1. `## Triads under Anti-Symmetric Ties` (Null, One-tie, Line).
2. `## Anti-Symmetric Star & Closed Triads` (Out-star, In-star, Cycle, Transitive Hierarchy).

### Step 1.3: Enlarge Nodes in the Hierarchies Diagram
On the `## Hierarchies: A Visual Example` slide:
* Increase node point size to `size = 35` (from `size = 32`).
* Decrease node text label size slightly to `size = 5.2` (from `size = 5.5`) to ensure names like `"President"`, `"VP 1"`, and `"VP 2"` fit perfectly inside the larger circle points.
* Widen the start and end edge caps to `circle(12, 'mm')` (from `circle(11, 'mm')`) to prevent directed arrows from overlapping with the node boundaries.
