### Step 1.3: Average Degrees (Means)
We will expand the section `### Average Degree in Directed Graphs` (under `## Directed Graph Metrics`):
- Explicitly define the **Mean Indegree** ($\bar{k}^{in}$) and **Mean Outdegree** ($\bar{k}^{out}$).
- Explain why they are mathematically guaranteed to be identical:
  $$
    \bar{k}^{in} = \bar{k}^{out} = \bar{k} = \frac{m}{n}
  $$
  This is because every directed edge has exactly one sender (contributing to outdegree) and one receiver (contributing to indegree), making the sums of both sets exactly equal to the total number of edges $m$.
