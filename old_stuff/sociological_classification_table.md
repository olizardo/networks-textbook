| Category | Type                 | Description                                                                 | Examples                                                                   |
|:---------|:---------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------|:---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **States**   | **Similarities**     | Connections based on having something in common; typically symmetric.       | Propinquity (same school), Memberships (same club), Attributes (same gender). |
|          | **Social Relations** | Connections based on a culturally recognized role or an affective/cognitive attitude. | **Roles**: Kinship (sibling, parent), Non-Kin (friend, boss). <br> **Attitudes**: Affective (like, hate), Cognitive (knows, is aware of). |
| **Events**   | **Interactions**     | Regular communication or behavior directed at another person; usually asymmetric. | Communications (texting, calling), Social Support (advice, help), Dominance (bullying, fighting). |
|          | **Flows**            | Transmission of something from one person to another; inherently directed.    | Information (gossip, influence), Beliefs, Resources (economic transactions). |
